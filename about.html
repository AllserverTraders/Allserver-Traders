Cash Back & Student Loans

Secure cash withdrawal services and short-term student loans with clear, transparent repayment agreements.

  • Cash withdrawal services
  • Student cash loans available
  • 35% interest for amounts less than R600
  • 30% interest for amounts greater than R600
  • Full repayment required within 14 days
  • Written agreement required before funds are released
All loans are short-term student loans. Borrowers must provide valid student details and agree in writing to repay the full amount plus interest within 14 days. Failure to repay within the agreed period may result in restricted future access to services.
